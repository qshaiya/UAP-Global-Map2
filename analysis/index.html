Methods and limitations

Records load live from data/sightings.json. Weather uses Open-Meteo historical archive at the stored coordinates and exact report date, if available. It is a modeled weather estimate, not direct evidence of the reported event. Location, source, date and confidence values come from the map database and are not independently validated. Reporting bias and uneven coverage mean counts are not sighting rates. No scheduled searches or paid AI calls are made by this page.
